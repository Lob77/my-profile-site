About Me

A calm, structured approach to learning Korean — built for adult learners who want real results.

Hi, I'm OOO — a Korean language teacher based in Seoul. For the past seven years, I've helped international students move from hesitant beginners to confident Korean speakers, whether their goal is daily conversation, university life, work, or the TOPIK exam.

My teaching philosophy is simple: every lesson should feel structured, encouraging, and immediately useful. I design each curriculum around the learner — not a fixed textbook — and track progress openly so you always know where you stand.
